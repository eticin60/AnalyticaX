What does AnalyticaX analyze?

Beyond indicators: AnalyticaX maps structure, liquidity runs, fair value gaps, trend shifts, and pattern confluences to create scenario-based analysis.

Structure & trend

Detects HH/HL and LH/LL swings, CHoCH/BOS, trend direction and where a trend is likely to fail or reverse.

Example: “Daily trend is down, but 1H is in corrective upside leg.”

Liquidity & stop hunts

Identifies equal highs/lows, stop hunt wicks, thin areas and liquidity pools that price tends to revisit.

Example: “Recent wicks above 95K cleared long liquidity, downside liquidity remains untouched.”

Patterns & risk frames

Spots wedges, triangles, flags, double tops/bottoms and harmonics; maps invalidation and risk zones.

Example: “Bull flag inside 4H uptrend – invalid below 94K close.”

How AnalyticaX works

You don’t need to connect your exchange. Just upload chart screenshots and let AnalyticaX translate technical noise into structured, human-readable reports.

Step 1
Prepare your chart

Open any exchange or TradingView chart, draw your levels and zones, and take a clean screenshot.

Step 2
Upload & select timeframe

Upload the image, choose the timeframe (1m → 1W) and the pair. AnalyticaX converts candles and markings into machine-readable data.

Step 3
AI generates scenarios

Step 4
Position review (optional)
