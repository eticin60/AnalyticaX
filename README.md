# AnalyticaX
AI-Powered Crypto Chart Insights. Not Analysis, AnalyticaX

AnalyticaX is an advanced AI-powered crypto chart interpretation platform that analyzes cryptocurrency screenshots.

The system automatically extracts trend structure, price behavior, candlestick patterns, liquidity areas, volatility zones, and potential market scenarios from the uploaded chart.

AnalyticaX goes beyond traditional indicators and combines both technical analysis principles and AI forecasting models.
Each analysis results:

Trend direction

Short- and medium-term probability percentages

Support and resistance levels

Risk scenario assessment

A clear, simple, and understandable interpretation

is presented to the user.

The platform is for informational purposes only; it does not provide investment advice or perform automated trading.
